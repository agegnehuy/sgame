# Kidy Road Safety Learning Game
## First-Time User Experience
### What a Child Sees When They Open the Game for the Very First Time

---

> This document describes every screen, every word, and every moment a child experiences
> the **first time** they ever open the Kidy Road Safety Learning Game.
> Nothing is assumed. Everything is explained.

---

## The Moment the Game Opens

The child taps the Kidy game icon on their Android phone.

The screen goes black for a moment. Then a soft animation appears — the Kidy logo fades in from the center of the screen. A short, friendly sound plays. The logo holds for two seconds, then fades out.

This is called the **Splash Screen**. It takes no more than 3 seconds.

---

## Screen 1 — Language Selection

**What the child sees:**

The screen is clean and bright. A large friendly question appears at the top:

> **"Choose your language"**
> **"ቋንቋዎን ይምረጡ"**

Both lines appear at the same time — English on top, Amharic below it. This way, no matter which language the child reads, they understand what the screen is asking.

Below the question are two large, colorful buttons side by side:

| Left Button | Right Button |
|-------------|--------------|
| **English** | **አማርኛ** |
| (with a flag or icon) | (with a flag or icon) |

The buttons are very large — easy to tap with a child's finger.

**What happens when the child taps a button:**

The chosen language is saved immediately. From this moment on, every word in the entire game will appear in the language the child selected. The screen transitions smoothly to the next screen.

**This screen appears only once** — the very first time the game is opened. After that, the game always opens in the child's chosen language.

---

## Screen 2 — Welcome Screen

**What the child sees:**

A warm, welcoming screen appears. In the center is a cheerful illustration — a cartoon child character standing on a sidewalk next to a zebra crossing, with a bright traffic light visible in the background.

Large, bold text appears above the illustration:

> **"Welcome to Safe Steps Addis!"**

Below the illustration, a single sentence:

> **"Let's learn how to stay safe on the road."**

At the bottom of the screen, one big button:

> **[ Let's Go! ]**

The button has a bright green color. It pulses gently to invite the child to tap it.

**What happens when the child taps "Let's Go!":**

The game moves to the profile creation screen.

---

## Screen 3 — Create Your Profile

**What the child sees:**

The screen shows a simple, fun form. At the top:

> **"Who are you?"**

**Step A — Enter your name:**

A text field appears with placeholder text inside it:

> *"Type your name here..."*

A keyboard appears on the screen. The child types their name.

**Step B — Choose your character:**

Below the name field, the screen shows 4 cartoon character options displayed in a row — all child-sized figures. Each one looks slightly different (different hair, different clothing color). The child taps one to select it. A bright border appears around the selected character to confirm the choice.

**Step C — Confirm:**

At the bottom, a button:

> **[ Start Playing ]**

If the child has not typed a name, this button stays grey and cannot be tapped. A small message appears:

> *"Please enter your name to continue."*

Once a name is entered and a character is chosen, the button turns green and the child can proceed.

**What happens when the child taps "Start Playing":**

The game saves the child's name and character to the device. This profile is now stored. The next time the child opens the game, it will go directly to the Home Screen with their name and character already loaded. The game transitions to the Tutorial.

---

## Screen 4 — Tutorial Introduction

**What the child sees:**

The screen dims slightly and a friendly message box appears in the center. A cartoon version of the child's chosen character stands beside it, pointing at the text. The message reads:

> **"Before you play, let's learn the rules of the road!"**
> **"It will only take a few minutes."**

Below the message, one button:

> **[ Start Tutorial ]**

And a small text link below it:

> *"Skip (not recommended)"*

The skip option is available but visually small and de-emphasized — the game strongly encourages completing the tutorial.

---

## Screen 5 — Tutorial Step 1: Move Your Character

**What the child sees:**

The game world appears for the first time. The child sees their character standing on a sidewalk. The road is visible ahead. There are no cars yet. Everything is still and quiet.

A large arrow appears on the screen pointing upward, and a glowing circle appears at the bottom-left corner — this is the **joystick control**.

A friendly instruction text bubble floats at the top of the screen:

> **"Use the circle to walk forward!"**

**What the child does:**

The child places their thumb on the glowing circle and drags it upward. Their character begins to walk forward on the sidewalk.

A gentle positive sound plays. The instruction bubble updates:

> **"Great job! Keep going!"**

The child walks until they reach the edge of the sidewalk, just before the road.

---

## Screen 6 — Tutorial Step 2: Stop at the Crossing

**What the child sees:**

The character reaches the edge of the sidewalk. A painted zebra crossing is visible on the road ahead. The character automatically stops.

A new instruction bubble appears:

> **"This is a Zebra Crossing. Always cross the road here — it is the safest place!"**

A glowing highlight appears around the zebra crossing stripes on the road, drawing the child's attention to it.

The child does not need to do anything yet — they just read and observe. After 3 seconds, a **"Next"** button appears at the bottom of the screen.

The child taps **"Next"** to continue.

---

## Screen 7 — Tutorial Step 3: Look at the Traffic Light

**What the child sees:**

The child's character is still at the edge of the sidewalk. Now, a traffic light post comes into view on the left side of the crossing. The light is currently **RED**.

The traffic light glows brightly. An arrow animation points to it. The instruction bubble says:

> **"This is a Traffic Light."**
> **"RED means STOP. Do not cross!"**

The red light pulses to emphasize it. A red circle animation radiates outward from the light.

Then a second line appears in the bubble:

> **"GREEN means GO. It is safe to cross!"**

The light slowly changes to green as the child watches, so they see both colors explained in sequence.

After the demonstration, the instruction bubble says:

> **"Now wait for the real green light before crossing."**

---

## Screen 8 — Tutorial Step 4: Wait for Green

**What the child sees:**

The traffic light resets to **RED**. Cars begin moving across the road — they drive past from left to right. The child's character stands still at the sidewalk.

An instruction bubble says:

> **"Wait here. Watch the traffic light."**

A small animated timer bar appears below the instruction. The child watches and waits. After a few seconds, the cars slow down and stop. The traffic light changes to **GREEN**.

A bright sound plays — like a gentle chime. Confetti-like sparkles appear around the green light. The instruction bubble updates:

> **"The light is GREEN! It is safe to cross now!"**

An arrow appears, pointing toward the zebra crossing.

---

## Screen 9 — Tutorial Step 5: Cross the Road

**What the child sees:**

The road is clear. The cars are stopped. The light is green. The child's character is ready at the crossing.

Two large buttons appear at the bottom of the screen:

| Left Button | Right Button |
|-------------|--------------|
| **WAIT** | **CROSS** |
| (yellow/orange) | (green) |

The instruction bubble says:

> **"The light is green and cars have stopped. What do you do?"**

**What the child does:**

The child taps **CROSS**.

Their character begins walking across the zebra crossing. A walking animation plays. The character moves step by step across the road, reaching the other side safely.

A celebratory animation plays — stars burst outward from the character. A sound of applause or a happy chime plays.

The instruction bubble shows:

> **"EXCELLENT! You crossed safely!"**

---

## Screen 10 — Tutorial Complete

**What the child sees:**

The screen transitions to a celebration screen. Confetti falls from the top. The child's character jumps and waves happily.

A large medal or star badge is shown in the center.

Text appears:

> **"Tutorial Complete!"**
> **"You know how to cross the road safely!"**

Below the medal, a summary of what was learned:

> ✅ Traffic lights — RED means stop, GREEN means go
> ✅ Zebra crossings — always use them
> ✅ Wait for cars to stop before crossing
> ✅ Make the right decision and cross safely

At the bottom, one big green button:

> **[ Start Playing! ]**

**What happens when the child taps "Start Playing!":**

The tutorial is marked as complete in the save file. The child will never see the tutorial again when they reopen the game. The screen transitions to the **Main Home Screen** — the real starting point of the game.

---

## Screen 11 — Home Screen (The Game Begins)

**What the child sees — for the first time — after completing all of the above:**

The Home Screen is now fully revealed. This is the main hub of the game. It contains:

**At the top:**
- The child's name and chosen character avatar (showing their profile)
- Their current coin count (showing 0 coins for a first-time player)
- A language toggle button (EN / AM)

**In the center:**
- The game title: **"Safe Steps Addis"**
- A mission map showing 5 mission slots labeled **M1, M2, M3, M4, M5**
- M1 is bright green and unlocked. M2–M5 are grey and locked
- A large green **"PLAY MISSION"** button below the map

**At the bottom:**
- Four navigation buttons: Store, Avatar, Reports, Exit

The child is now ready to play their first real mission.

## Summary: The Complete First-Time Flow

```
OPEN GAME (tap icon)
       ↓
SPLASH SCREEN (Kidy logo, 3 seconds)
       ↓
LANGUAGE SELECTION (English or Amharic)
       ↓
WELCOME SCREEN ("Welcome to Safe Steps Addis!")
       ↓
CREATE PROFILE (name + choose character)
       ↓
TUTORIAL INTRO ("Let's learn the rules!")
       ↓
TUTORIAL STEP 1 — Move your character
       ↓
TUTORIAL STEP 2 — Stop at the zebra crossing
       ↓
TUTORIAL STEP 3 — Learn the traffic light
       ↓
TUTORIAL STEP 4 — Wait for green
       ↓
TUTORIAL STEP 5 — Cross the road safely
       ↓
TUTORIAL COMPLETE (celebration screen)
       ↓
HOME SCREEN — The game begins
       ↓
SELECT MISSION 1 — First real gameplay
```

**Total time for the first-time experience:** Approximately 3 to 5 minutes.

---

## What Happens the Second Time the Child Opens the Game

The second time the child opens the game, they skip everything above and go directly to:

> **Splash Screen → Home Screen**

Their name, character, coins, and progress are already saved. The game remembers them. They pick up exactly where they left off.
